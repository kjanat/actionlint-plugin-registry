About this catalogue

Publisher identities and artifact checksums are verified during admission. Acceptance does not guarantee that a plugin is safe or correct. Release status data lists deprecations, withdrawals and revocations.

Downloads are GitHub's cumulative counters for accepted assets, including CI, validation and repeat downloads. They do not measure unique users or installations. Cached checks do not download again. Unavailable or unsupported counts are not zero.
