About this catalogue

Publisher identities and artifact checksums are verified during admission. Acceptance does not guarantee that a plugin is safe or correct. Release status data lists deprecations, withdrawals and revocations.

Downloads are GitHub's cumulative counters for the accepted assets. They include CI, validation and repeat downloads; they do not measure unique users or installations. Cached checks do not download again. Unavailable or unsupported counts are not zero.
