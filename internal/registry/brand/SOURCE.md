Copied without modification from `kjanat/actionlint`, commit
`51c4b21a2f9ef503fa857c9a0a5099cad981fb76`, `docs/assets/brand/`:

- `actionlint.svg`: horizontal logo for light backgrounds.
- `actionlint-dark.svg`: horizontal logo for dark backgrounds.
- `actionlint-mark.svg`: standalone mark.
- `LICENSE.md`: original branding licence.

Source: <https://github.com/kjanat/actionlint/tree/51c4b21a2f9ef503fa857c9a0a5099cad981fb76/docs/assets/brand>

Artwork is unmodified. SVG attribution metadata is retained.
Horizontal logos render at 260 pixels wide; the standalone mark is used on
narrow screens. The mark renders at 40 pixels with 5 pixels of clear space;
horizontal logos have at least one eighth of their symbol width clear around them.
